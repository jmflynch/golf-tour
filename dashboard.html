How it went

Strokes over par, hole by hole. Lower is better; the tick is the turn.

Percocet Tiger had this round by the throat from the 6th hole to the 13th, then lost it in about twenty minutes.

San Tan was survivable for everyone but Mijo Vato, who opened 7-6-6-5 and stood at +9 before the round had really started. Percocet Tiger turned in 39, Phil Dickelson 42, Pink Starburst 44 — and Pink Starburst's 44 was a 38 with two haymakers buried in it, a 7 on the 3rd and an 8 on the 481-yard 8th. That 8th is the number one handicap hole and it took a piece out of all four of them; nobody made par.

Superstition is where it turned. Percocet Tiger gave a stroke back on the opening par 4 and never found it again, then made 5 on a 105-yard par 3 at the 14th and the lead was gone. Pink Starburst played the nine in +1 with birdies at the 11th and the 15th — the only two red numbers on the entire card — took sole possession at the 14th, and was never caught.

Pink Starburst 77. Percocet Tiger 81. Phil Dickelson 82. Mijo Vato 88.
